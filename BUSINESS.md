# BUSINESS.md — BeatTheATS

## What it is

A free-to-try, one-time-payment web tool: paste your resume + a job description, get an instant ATS (Applicant Tracking System) compatibility score, see exactly which keywords are missing, and unlock a full gap-closing report for a small one-time fee.

## STEP 6: Validation

**1. Kto dokładnie kupuje?**
Active job seekers applying online right now — mostly white-collar/office roles (tech, marketing, admin, finance, customer support) where employers use ATS software (Workday, Greenhouse, Taleo, iCIMS). Global, English-language resumes first (largest ATS-using market: US, UK, Canada, Australia, India). Age skews 22–40, applying to multiple jobs per week, frustrated after several rejections with no feedback.

**2. Jaki problem rozwiązujemy?**
75%+ of large employers use ATS software that auto-filters resumes before a human ever reads them. Candidates get silently rejected with zero feedback and don't know if the problem is their skills, their resume's keywords/formatting, or both. This creates real anxiety and wasted effort (submitting the same flawed resume to 50 jobs).

**3. Dlaczego ktoś miałby zapłacić?**
Because the alternative is: keep guessing, keep getting silently rejected, or pay a career coach $100–300/hr. We give a concrete, specific, instant answer ("you're missing these 12 keywords from this exact job ad") for a fraction of the cost of a single coaching session. The free score creates the itch ("62/100 — see what's wrong"); the paid report scratches it.

**4. Ile może zapłacić?**
One-time unlock: **$9 (≈36 PLN)** per report (per job application, or per "pack" of analyses). Comparable paid tool Jobscan charges $50/month subscription — we're a fraction of that for people who just need to fix *this one* application. Anchor price validated by direct competitor.

**5. Jak znajdziemy pierwszych 10 użytkowników?**
Manual, zero-budget: post the free tool link (no login required) in r/jobs, r/resumes, r/ApplyingToCollege-adjacent career subs, and relevant LinkedIn/Facebook job-search groups, framed as "I built a free tool that tells you why ATS is rejecting your resume — feedback welcome." Genuine value-first post, not an ad. Target: 10 real users within the first 48 hours of posting.

**6. Jak znajdziemy pierwszych 100?**
Double down on the channel that produced the first paying customer(s): if Reddit converts, post in 5–10 more relevant subreddits over 2 weeks (career, resume, specific-industry subs) + start a short-form content angle ("3 reasons ATS is rejecting your resume" as a TikTok/Reels/Shorts hook driving to the free tool) + light SEO (the tool page itself targets "ATS resume checker free" long-tail search).

**7. Dlaczego ktoś wybierze nas zamiast alternatywy?**
- vs. **doing nothing**: instant, specific, free-to-start feedback beats silence.
- vs. **Jobscan ($50/mo subscription)**: we're one-time and $9 — no commitment for someone who just needs to fix one or two applications right now.
- vs. **a career coach**: instant and 1/20th the price.
- vs. **generic "resume AI writer" tools**: we don't rewrite your resume generically — we score it against *this specific job ad* you're applying to, which is a sharper, more credible value prop.

**Verdict: validation is strong enough to build — proceeding to MVP.**

## Monetization

- Model: **freemium → one-time payment** (per-report unlock).
- Price: **$9 / 36 PLN** per unlocked full report.
- Upsell path (not in v1, roadmap only): 3-pack for $19, or "unlimited re-checks for this job search" subscription at $15/month once volume justifies building it.
